✓ Verified BlockchainReporter; ForkLog · Oct 25, 2026 · source

Electrum wallet fixes security flaw after hackers steal Bitcoin
◆ Single-source crypto.news · Oct 25, 2026 · source
All Electrum versions before 4.8.3 were exposed to a high-severity flaw in which malicious servers swapped unconfirmed transactions for unsigned fakes and tricked users into approving a Replace-by-Fee bump that paid the attacker, with at least 1 confirmed theft and 2 reported incidents. Why it matters: The attack relied on server trust and address-lookalike matching of the last four characters, so light-wallet users on default public servers carry hidden counterparty risk. Loss amount undisclosed. The advisory dated Oct 24 is a primary source but was not opened, so the tier is capped.
