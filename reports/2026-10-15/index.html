FinCEN Kills Two Proposed Surveillance Rules Targeting Self-Custody and Mixers
✓ Verified TFTC (Unchained independently reports it and quotes FinCEN; FinCEN and the Federal Register were not opened) · Oct 5, 2026 (outside the 7-day window) · source
