SIGNAL 01

88% of Bitget's stolen funds sit dormant as tracers find CoinJoin laundering, hours ahead of phased withdrawal resumption

Moved
Compliance firm AMLBot traced a slice of the Sep 24 Bitget breach (final tally $387.5M, revised up from $351.6M as more drained wallets were identified) through a TRON→USDT→Ethereum→THORChain→BTC path into a Wasabi CoinJoin privacy pool, while about 88% of tracked proceeds ($343M) remains unmoved across 13 attacker wallets. Circle and Tether have frozen only $318,000 of the total so far. Bitget has scheduled a phased withdrawal resumption beginning Sep 28, backstopped by its $464M+ User Protection Fund.
Matters
Attackers routing through an open-source CoinJoin pool rather than a centralized mixer complicates the sanctions/freeze playbook regulators have relied on since Tornado Cash — issuer freezes only work on the fraction of funds still held as USDT/USDC, which here is a rounding error against the ETH and BTC the exploiter can still move. TRM Labs data cited across coverage says North Korea-linked actors were responsible for nearly three-quarters of all crypto stolen in 2026, making this the latest data point in a now-dominant threat pattern rather than an isolated incident.
Next
Sep 28's phased withdrawal resumption is the first concrete test of whether Bitget's recovery plan holds without a further loss revision or a run on the User Protection Fund. Separately, watch whether a second forensics shop (Chainalysis, Elliptic) independently confirms AMLBot's specific tracing findings — only one firm has published this route so far.

SIGNAL 03

The Federal Reserve proposes its first GENIUS Act stablecoin rules — full reserves, no yield to holders

Moved
The Fed proposed two rules on Sep 24 translating the GENIUS Act into bank-supervision practice: full reserve/HQLA backing with no interest or yield paid to holders, plus a tailored bank application process for issuing payment stablecoins, opening a 60-day public comment window. Governor Michael Barr's accompanying statement flagged AML-alignment concerns and said “further work will undoubtedly be required.”
Matters
This is the first concrete Fed rulemaking translating GENIUS Act text into operational rules, and the no-yield-to-holders line sets up a two-tier landscape between bank-issued stablecoins and the yield-bearing products (USDe, sUSDS) booming outside the banking perimeter. It landed the same week Binance put $100M of its own balance sheet behind USDC via a renewed five-year distribution deal — regulation, distribution and consumer-trust levers are all being pulled simultaneously right now.
Next
The Fed's comment period runs to roughly Nov 23, 2026 (60 days from Sep 24); Treasury's separate Section 3 issuance NPRM comment period closes Oct 19, 2026. Final rules from these two tracks will set the operating terms for every bank-issued stablecoin ahead of GENIUS's January 2027 effective date.

◆ Single-source 24/7 Wall St. (syndicated to Yahoo Finance, not independent) · Sep 26, 2026 · source

  • Losses remain concentrated in a small number of mega-incidents — September's roughly $684M total is dominated by essentially two events (Bitget ~$387.5M, the earlier Liquid Network exploit), making monthly “loss total” headlines misleading proxies for broad protocol security.
