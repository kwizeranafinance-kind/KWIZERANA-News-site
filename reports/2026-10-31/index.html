Inside the largest crypto hack on record: How attackers breached Binance for $6.3 billion
✓ Verified The Block; CoinDesk; Help Net Security · Oct 30, 2026 · source
TRM says the attacker staged for at least 36 hours via Tornado Cash-funded wallets, then used EIP-7702 to turn a hot wallet into a sweeper with 50 pre-signed authorizations, with valid signatures on other chains within about 30 minutes.
