Banks stopped waiting for stablecoin rails and started building two of them

Moved
Twenty-one institutions — Bank of America, Citi, Goldman Sachs, Wells Fargo, UBS, Deutsche, Santander, BBVA, MUFG, Fidelity, PNC, TD and others — committed on Sep 1 to form a company in H2 2026 for a joint USD stablecoin live in H1 2027. Matters
Two rival bank formats appeared in one week, and they differ on the thing that matters fiscally: tokenized deposits stay on bank balance sheets and generate no Treasury-bill demand, while stablecoins under the GENIUS reserve mandate do. The trillion-dollar marginal buyer of US government debt that Treasury Secretary Bessent has pointed to only materialises if the stablecoin format wins — and with supply stalled at $312B rather than compounding, that front-end bid is a policy assumption, not an observed flow.
Next
