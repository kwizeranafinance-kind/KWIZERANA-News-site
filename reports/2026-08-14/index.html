Key Trends

  • The bridge is the bug. Kelp, Aztec, Harmony — three different architectures, same failure point. Cross-chain custody is where 2026's money actually leaves.
  • Cold storage assumptions are being repriced. Coldcard proved that entropy at key-generation is a bigger risk than network exposure. "Offline" was never the whole threat model.
  • ZK is graduating from privacy to plumbing. SecondFi's proof-of-ownership refund portal shows ZK's near-term product-market fit is compliance and remediation, not anonymity.

Ones to Watch

  • SecondFi's September ZK portal — if it works, expect ZK claim rails to become standard in hack recovery, and a new category of infrastructure startups to chase it.
