First quantum-resistant Bitcoin transaction confirmed on mainnet — no soft fork
The Block / The Quantum Insider · Aug 26–27, 2026 · source
StarkWare researcher Avihu Levy's Quantum-Safe Bitcoin adds a hash-based lock in parallel with the existing signature check, using signature grinding to avoid exposing public key data. Cost was $150–200 and hours of GPU work, and because ordinary nodes will not relay the non-standard format, it had to be handed directly to a willing miner.
Quantum defence on Bitcoin no longer requires a contentious fork — it requires money and a miner relationship. That is a very different threat model for anyone holding long-dated cold storage.
The Sandbox post-mortem: 14.74M SAND drained through a bridge config flaw
Bitcoin.com News / Blockonomi · Aug 28, 2026 · source
The attacker minted unbacked SAND on Base and BNB Smart Chain and pulled 14,742,341.84 SAND from the Ethereum adapter in under a minute for roughly 80 ETH ($675K). Total economic damage ~$1.5M, ~$987K extracted. Ethereum and Polygon holdings were untouched; pre-breach wallets get a 1:1 replacement.
Multi-chain deployments multiply the config surface, not just the audience. The token contract was fine on two chains and fatally misconfigured on two others.

Key Trends

  • Nobody is breaking the math. Every meaningful loss this week came from a quorum, a bridge config, or an authorization check. Audits that only prove contract correctness are auditing the wrong thing.
  • Post-quantum moved from paper to mainnet. QSB proves the defence exists today — but at $150–200 a transaction and requiring out-of-band miner relay, it is a bespoke service, not a standard.
  • Shutting down is now an acceptable incident response. BounceBit killed its L1 outright. Expect more chains to migrate to shared security rather than defend their own.

Ones to Watch

  • Whether QSB gets a standard relay path. Until nodes propagate the format natively, quantum-safe Bitcoin remains available only to holders who can negotiate directly with miners — a privacy and censorship problem of its own.

Key Trends

  • Headline totals are debt and token contributions in disguise. $1.298B becomes ~$23M of real equity once ZeroStack's non-cash $1B and Ripple's $275M of notes are stripped out.
  • The biggest cheques are going to power and compute. Core Scientific $3.3B, IREN $3.55B of convertibles. Crypto's largest 2026 raises are financing AI data centres.
  • Fewer, larger, later. Deal count down 46% on the year while round sizes grow. Early-stage DeFi is back to $1.5M–$2.5M seeds with no token premium attached.

Ones to Watch

  • Convertible notes as the sector's dominant instrument. IREN and Core Scientific have issued roughly $7B between them against assets whose fallback use is mining a $77K bitcoin. If AI revenue slips, that maturity wall becomes everyone's problem.
  • Security as a fundable category. Hypernative's $40M lands in the same week as four exploits. If on-chain threat detection can raise growth-stage money, the incident rate has become an insurable market rather than a tail risk.
