Coldcard hardware wallet theft passes $130M as a fifth wave hits
TechCrunch · Aug 4, 2026 (ongoing) · source
Firmware shipped from March 2021 generated wallet seeds from a deterministic pseudo-random generator instead of the hardware RNG, making thousands of Coinkite Coldcard seeds derivable offline.
The worst class of failure there is: cold storage, correct user behaviour, silent compromise. Any KWIZERANA treasury seed created on affected firmware must be rotated, not audited.
Term Finance governance takeover confirms the DAO attack pattern
The Crypto Times · Aug 23, 2026 · source
$8.5M seized through legitimate proposals passed by an attacker holding a majority of an illiquid governance token.
Nothing was hacked. Every transaction was valid. That is precisely why the mitigations are political, not technical.
SafePal plug-in flaw exposes 39,798 customers' names and addresses
Fairyproof security watch · Aug 2026 · source
An authorization flaw in an order-tracking plug-in leaked names, emails, shipping addresses, phone numbers and purchase history for roughly forty thousand hardware wallet buyers.
A shipping list of confirmed hardware-wallet owners is a targeting list. Expect physical-coercion and phishing attempts against those names for months.
Structural & 7-Day
Seven governance takeovers logged across Ethereum, Solana and Base since June
Blockaid · Aug 2026 · source
$22M drained in total, including Panther, where a passing vote reached the protocol's upgrade rights, and Unicly, where voting power was flash-borrowed for a single block.
Flash-borrowed voting power means an attacker needs no capital at all — only a block. Snapshot-based voting without a lock period is now structurally unsafe.
Regulators shift from prosecuting code to prosecuting behaviour
CoinDesk Research · 2026 · source
After OFAC lifted sanctions on Tornado Cash — its first delisting of a protocol — enforcement has moved toward conduct-based cases, with new legislation rather than reinterpretation seen as the likely path.
This is the most favourable privacy environment since 2022. It is also fragile: a single high-profile laundering case reverses the posture.
Zcash ecosystem restructures into five organisations as institutional ZEC accumulates
CoinDesk · 2026 · source
The Zcash ecosystem split into five independent orgs in Q1, ZODL raised $25M privately, and Nasdaq-listed Cypherpunk Technologies (CYPH) accumulated over $90M of ZEC.
Privacy is being bought through public equity wrappers. That routes institutional demand around the exchange-delisting problem entirely — directly relevant to the true-privacy pillar.

Key Trends

  • The failures are supply-chain, not cryptographic. Coldcard was a five-year-old firmware RNG bug; SafePal was a plug-in. Neither was broken maths. Vendor process is the real threat model.
  • Governance capture is cheap and repeatable. Seven incidents, $22M, three chains, and at least one required no capital thanks to flash-borrowed votes. Expect copycats until quorum rules change.
  • Privacy is repricing upward. OFAC's Tornado delisting, the Zcash restructuring and a Nasdaq treasury holding $90M+ ZEC form a coherent thaw — the first in three years.

Ones to Watch

  • Coldcard remediation guidance from Coinkite. Whether affected users get a verified derivation-check tool determines how much of the remaining exposed balance gets moved before the next wave.
  • Flash-loan-resistant voting. The first major protocol to ship vote-locking or block-delay snapshots sets the template everyone copies. Worth reading the implementation when it lands.
