Last 7 Days
Coldcard hardware wallets drained via a five-year-old seed-entropy flaw
Fortune / Bloomberg / TechCrunch · Aug 3–4, 2026 · Fortune · TechCrunch
From July 30, an attacker exploited a firmware flaw in Coinkite’s Coldcard wallet-seed generation that reduced key randomness, making supposedly unreachable Bitcoin searchable. TechCrunch puts total theft across offline hardware wallets above $130M.
Air-gapped is not the same as secure. The threat model everyone bought a hardware wallet to defeat — remote attackers — was defeated anyway, because the entropy was weak at generation time. Cold storage is a custody choice, not a guarantee.
SafePal exposes ~39,798 customer records through an order-tracking plug-in
Fairyproof Weekly Security Watch · Aug 2026 · fairyproof.substack.com
An authorization flaw in a third-party order-tracking plug-in exposed names, email addresses, shipping addresses, phone numbers and purchase details for roughly 39,798 SafePal customers.
This is a physical-security incident dressed as a data breach. A list of confirmed hardware-wallet owners with home addresses and purchase values is exactly the targeting data used for wrench attacks. Treat it more seriously than the record count suggests.
Practitioner response guidance published for post-Coldcard hardware wallet holders
Shattered.io · Aug 2026 · shattered.io
A 12-step remediation guide circulated for holders of affected devices, covering seed regeneration on known-good entropy, staged migration, and verification of destination addresses.
The migration itself is the risky step — moving funds under time pressure is exactly when address-poisoning and clipboard malware land. Do it slowly, verify on-device, and test with a small amount first.

Key Trends

  • Cold storage lost its assumption of safety this month. $130M+ pulled from air-gapped devices via generation-time entropy weakness. The security boundary moved from “is it online” to “was the key generated correctly, on which firmware version.” Every hardware wallet in the DAO needs a firmware-version audit.
  • The attack surface is operational software, not contracts. Metabase at Bits of Gold, an order-tracking plug-in at SafePal. Neither was on-chain. Crypto firms audit Solidity and run unpatched self-hosted BI tools next to their customer database.
  • Old bugs are new bugs now. A five-year-old flaw found and weaponized within days once tooling improved. Point-in-time audits have a shrinking shelf life; continuous testing is becoming the only defensible posture.

Ones to Watch

  • Whether the remaining Coldcard funds move. Most of the 1,816 BTC is still pooled and unlaundered. Movement into mixers or a fresh liquidation wave would be the signal that recovery odds have collapsed — and could pressure BTC order books.
  • Regulatory response to hardware-wallet disclosure standards. A five-year-old entropy flaw in a self-custody device is the strongest argument yet for mandated firmware disclosure — and, unhelpfully, for the case against self-custody. Expect this to be cited in GENIUS Act comment letters.
