Fincen scraps crypto mixer rule built to fight ransomware
✓ Verified American Banker (Oct 6); The Block (Oct 5); TFTC (Oct 5) · Oct 6, 2026 · source
FinCEN withdrew its Oct 2023 mixer proposal, which would have covered about 15,000 institutions and 1.47M annual reporting hours, and the 2020 self-hosted wallet rule, citing a 'chilling effect on legitimate activity'. Why it matters: It removes a six-year US threat to mixers and self-custody, though TFTC notes a future administration could re-propose and existing BSA duties and OFAC sanctions are unchanged. The date is Oct 5 in The Block and TFTC and Oct 6 in American Banker and the Federal Register.
ZachXBT says he fronted $349,700 to infiltrate alleged Chinese launderers tied to Lazarus, Bybit hack
◆ Single-source The Block; the claims are ZachXBT's own · Oct 6, 2026 · source
ZachXBT says he put up 349,700 USDC posing as a client to infiltrate a network linked to the $1.5B 2025 Bybit hack, and credits his work with helping freeze about $75M of DPRK-related funds since 2022. Why it matters: Laundering runs through OTC brokers, so freezes at stablecoin issuers and exchanges are where recovery happens. The laundering allegations are unconfirmed.
Conduit sues Tether over $2.76 million freeze, says it has 'no legal entitlement' to funds
◆ Single-source The Block, relaying the plaintiff's complaint · Oct 6, 2026 · source
Conduit filed in SDNY over $2.76M of USDT frozen for more than a year after a Brazilian Federal Police matter involving a former customer, and seeks $2.76M in damages. Why it matters: It tests how much due process a stablecoin issuer owes when it freezes funds on a third-party probe. The allegations are one-sided.
