Coldcard damage assessment lands: $130M+ gone, and firmware updates cannot undo it
TechCrunch / Fortune · Aug 3–10, 2026 · fortune.com
A build-configuration error in a March 2021 Coldcard firmware release caused seed generation to fall back on a weak software RNG, reducing key entropy enough to make some wallets brute-forceable.
Every wallet created under the affected firmware is permanently compromised. Patching protects new seeds only — the required action is generate new seed, move funds, no exceptions.

Key Trends

  • Entropy is the new attack surface. Coldcard and the Zcash Orchard counterfeiting bug were both failures at the cryptographic-primitive layer, not the application layer. Audits that stop at contract logic miss this class entirely.
  • Privacy is decoupling from illicit-finance framing. Zcash shipping verifiable supply, ETF filings, and the Storm split verdict together push privacy toward a compliance-compatible posture rather than an adversarial one.
  • Time-to-exploit is collapsing. Dormant vulnerabilities are being industrialised in days. Continuous testing is becoming table stakes, not a maturity-model aspiration.

Ones to Watch

  • Coldcard restitution and Coinkite's legal exposure. No hardware wallet maker has ever faced liability at this scale. Whatever precedent emerges will set the standard for the entire self-custody industry.
  • Privacy coin ETF filings. If any shielded-asset ETF clears review, it is the definitive proof that the regulatory posture toward privacy has flipped — and the strongest possible tailwind for the privacy pillar.
