Coldcard hack traced to a five-year-old firmware entropy failure
TRM Labs / Fortune / TechCrunch · Aug 3–4, 2026 · trmlabs.com · TechCrunch
Starting July 30, an attacker drained BTC from Coldcards whose seeds were generated after a March 2021 firmware release. A build configuration error caused seed generation to fall back to a weak software RNG instead of the hardware entropy source.
Air-gapped did not help, because the compromise was in key generation, not in signing. If you generated a seed on a Coldcard after March 2021, the threat model you thought you had never existed. Move funds to a freshly generated seed.
SafePal breach exposes 39,798 customers' names, addresses and purchase history
Northeast Times · Aug 17, 2026 · northeasttimes.com
An authorization flaw in an order-tracking plug-in exposed customer data for orders placed between March 2025 and April 2026. SafePal has since removed more than 30 fraudulent sites and phishing links tied to the leak.
This is a wrench-attack dataset, not a data-privacy inconvenience. A list of verified hardware-wallet buyers with home addresses is the single most dangerous artifact in this industry, and it is now circulating.
Stolen Coldcard funds barely laundered — one 64.9 BTC Wasabi deposit, 200 ETH to Tornado Cash
TRM Labs · Aug 2026 · trmlabs.com
Funds are pooling at a handful of attacker addresses with almost no layering. Total documented mixing so far is a single Wasabi deposit and one Tornado Cash transaction on August 4.
Either the attacker is patient and waiting for liquidity, or they lack a viable offramp. Both are informative. Watch those addresses — the moment layering starts is the moment the exit path becomes visible, and it will name a jurisdiction.

Key Trends

  • The supply chain is the attack surface now. Coldcard was a 2021 build config error. SafePal was a third-party plug-in. Neither was a chain-level or cryptographic break. The code you did not write is where the money leaves.
  • AI has collapsed the cost of targeting. 885,000 enumerated numbers at a 13.6% confirmation rate is industrial-scale reconnaissance that would have been uneconomic two years ago. Defence has to assume the attacker knows who you are.
  • Privacy tooling is legally viable again but operationally watched. Treasury's March 2026 report conceded mixers serve lawful purposes and the Tornado Cash sanctions were overturned — yet every mixer deposit in this hack is being tracked in public. Legal is not the same as private.

Ones to Watch

  • Whether the Coldcard attacker begins layering. ~$130M sitting unlaundered in a handful of addresses is unstable. The first serious mixing wave will tell you whether this was a state actor with an offramp or an opportunist who is now stuck.
  • Physical security follow-on from the SafePal list. Roughly 40,000 confirmed hardware-wallet owners with home addresses. If wrench-attack reports rise in the next 60 days, this leak is the likely source — and it changes how the industry treats e-commerce data.
