Coldcard hardware wallets drained of ~1,816 BTC via a five-year-old firmware flaw
TRM Labs / TechCrunch · Jul 30 – Aug 2026 · source
Beginning July 30, an attacker systematically drained bitcoin from affected Coinkite Coldcard devices by exploiting a long-dormant firmware bug. Galaxy Research traced roughly 1,816 BTC (~$116M) off those wallets; TechCrunch put the broader tally above $130M.
Cold storage is a device-supply-chain and firmware-update problem, not a solved one. Any KWIZERANA cold-storage policy needs a documented firmware-provenance and update procedure.
Zcash Orchard carried a counterfeiting bug from 2022 until May 2026
Medium / Zcash disclosures · June 2026 · source
A flaw in the cryptographic machinery protecting Zcash's shielded transaction integrity would in theory have allowed unlimited counterfeit ZEC to be created undetected. It was found in a targeted security review and patched via emergency upgrade before disclosure. Ecosystem disclosures state no evidence of exploitation.
The uncomfortable part of shielded pools: you cannot audit supply from outside. Privacy tech has to be trusted on proof quality alone, which raises the bar on who reviews it and how often.
Treasury concedes mixers have legitimate uses — while prosecutions of operators continue
CoinDesk · Mar 9, 2026 (standing policy) · source
A 32-page Treasury report to Congress acknowledged that mixers can lawfully shield personal, business and charitable transactions when paired with record-keeping safeguards. FinCEN nonetheless continues its Section 311 rulemaking on CVC mixing, and Samourai Wallet's founders pleaded guilty to unlicensed money transmission.
The protocol-level sanctions era is over; the operator-liability era is not. Privacy tooling is legal to use and still dangerous to run — a distinction that matters for anything KWIZERANA builds.

Key Trends

  • Dormant-flaw discovery is the pattern of 2026. Coldcard's bug was five years old, Zcash's four. The 2026 attack surface is code that has been "battle-tested" purely by not being looked at.
  • Policy has split from enforcement on privacy. Treasury says lawful users deserve on-chain privacy; prosecutors keep convicting the people who build the tools. Build accordingly.
  • Incident response is normalising around halts and rollbacks. Three halts in a week and one state rewrite. Institutional allocators will start asking chains to disclose their halt authority.

Ones to Watch

  • Tectonic's restitution accounting. Still unpublished. How the $68.7M of frozen funds is allocated will set the template for every future rollback-and-restore.
  • FinCEN's Section 311 CVC-mixing rule. If finalised as proposed, covered institutions must report suspected mixing activity — which would de facto re-restrict privacy tooling that Treasury's own March report defended.

Just over 300 deals have closed in 2026 against about 840 in the same period of 2025, but $8.1B has been invested versus $9.4B — roughly a 13% decline. Median deal size rose from $11.1M to $27.1M.
Same capital, one third the companies. Seed-stage crypto founders are facing a genuinely closed market while later-stage companies with product-market fit are getting bigger cheques than in 2025.

Miner AI/HPC contracts dwarf the entire crypto VC market
insights4vc · 2026 · source
Signed AI/HPC contracts by bitcoin miners now exceed $70B — IREN/Microsoft at $9.7B, TeraWulf at $12.8B, Core Scientific around $10B — against $8.1B of total crypto venture funding in 2026.
Nearly 9x more capital is flowing into crypto-adjacent compute infrastructure than into crypto software. That ratio is the clearest statement of where the market thinks the returns are.
The Block: crypto VC is maturing rather than contracting
The Block · Aug 11, 2026 · source
Coverage frames the drop in deal count against stable dollars as a maturation signal: more capital going to established companies with proven product-market fit, fewer speculative early cheques, and larger median rounds.
"Maturing" and "closed to newcomers" describe the same data. For a DAO like KWIZERANA, it argues for revenue-funded growth over a raise attempt in this window.

Key Trends

  • The barbell is now the whole market. Median deal size 2.4x'd to $27.1M while deal count fell 64%. Mega-rounds and nothing else.
  • Compute infrastructure is outraising crypto software ~9:1. >$70B in miner AI/HPC contracts against $8.1B of crypto VC. Capital is backing megawatts, not protocols.
  • Digital-asset-treasury vehicles are issuing at yields that require appreciation. A 13% dividend funded by SOL purchases is the clearest example of financing that only works in one direction.
