Key Trends

  • Old code is the biggest exposure. The Coldcard flaw was five years old before anyone weaponised it. Dormancy is not safety; it is an unexercised liability.
  • Attackers are not laundering. Coldcard proceeds sit unmixed at a few addresses. Either they are confident about jurisdiction or the off-ramp is already arranged — both are worse than layering.
  • Privacy tooling is being split from privacy developers. The contracts were delisted; the prosecution continues. Legal risk now attaches to authorship, not usage.

Ones to Watch
