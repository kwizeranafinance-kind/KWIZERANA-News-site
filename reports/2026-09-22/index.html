SIGNAL 01

▲ Avalanche's Helicon upgrade is set to activate as AVAX sells off 4.66% and C-Chain TVL pulls back from its five-day spike

Moved
This signal touches Avalanche C-Chain, the house chain. Helicon — Avalanche's biggest staking-economics change since genesis — is scheduled to activate on Mainnet today at 15:00 UTC (after this brief's compile time), cutting the minimum validator staking commitment from 14 days to 48 hours, adding auto-renewing validator staking, raising the reward-eligible uptime bar from 80% to 90%, bringing Continuous Execution to the C-Chain, and phasing the minimum staking-reward consumption rate from 10% down to 7.5% over 90 days (Avalanche's own Builder Hub blog, corroborated by the AvalancheGo v1.15.0 GitHub release). Heading into activation, AVAX fell 4.66% over the past 24 hours to $10.81 (CoinGecko, this brief's live pull), reversing a roughly 57% seven-day run-up; separately, this brief's own live DefiLlama pull shows Avalanche C-Chain TVL easing to $628.55M today, down 2.8% from Monday's $646.86M five-day peak though still up 35.5% versus the $463.98M read on Sep 16.
Matters
Shorter lockups and parallelized execution are a direct bid for institutional capital that couldn't previously commit to a two-week validator lockup, and for the C-Chain throughput DeFi protocols have cited as a constraint; Avalanche's own modeling projects roughly a 1.3-point APR trim on short-duration staking and a 0.5–1% cut to annual AVAX inflation. The AVAX price drop and the TVL pullback are both independently-pulled, verified data points; the “sell-the-news” explanation for them circulating in trade coverage is a single-source causal read, not a confirmed mechanism — the numbers and the narrative are graded separately.
Next
Whether AVAX stabilizes above the ~$10.50 support level some analysts are watching once Helicon actually activates at 15:00 UTC, or extends toward the $8.00–8.50 zone flagged as the next support below it; whether Avalanche C-Chain TVL resumes climbing now that shorter lockups and Continuous Execution are live, or whether today's ~$628M reading marks a stall rather than a pause.

ECB proposes scrapping MiCA's bank-deposit reserve rule for stablecoin issuers in favor of liquidity thresholds
✓ Verified Cointelegraph, corroborated by Cryptobriefing · Sep 22, 2026 · source
The European System of Central Banks formally proposed replacing MiCA's requirement that 30% (60% for “significant” stablecoins) of reserves sit in bank deposits with EBA-style liquidity thresholds — 40–60% convertible to cash within one to five business days for significant stablecoins, 20–30% for others.
If adopted, this reshapes reserve-composition rules for every euro-area stablecoin issuer, and signals regulators worry MiCA's current deposit mandate could itself trigger bank liquidity stress during a large redemption event.
