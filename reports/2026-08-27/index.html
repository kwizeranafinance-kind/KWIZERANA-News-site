Bitcoin researchers propose a quantum migration that would not crowd out transactions
CoinDesk · Aug 27, 2026 · source
The proposal addresses the core objection to post-quantum Bitcoin — that migrating millions of UTXOs to larger quantum-resistant signatures would flood blockspace — with a path that avoids displacing ordinary transaction throughput.
The quantum debate just moved from timeline speculation to blockspace engineering. When the argument becomes "how do we ship it without breaking fees", the assumption has already shifted to "we will ship it".
Ethereum developers propose a first step to protect ETH staking from quantum attacks
CoinDesk · Aug 26, 2026 · source
The proposal targets validator key material rather than user accounts — hardening the consensus layer first, where a compromise would be systemic rather than individual.
Both major chains published quantum work inside 24 hours of each other. With $24.10B in Lido alone, staking keys are the highest-value single target on Ethereum. Consensus-layer first is the correct ordering.
SEC revives the crypto custody rule the previous administration could not land
CoinDesk · Aug 26, 2026 · source
The Commission is bringing back a custody rulemaking that stalled under the prior administration, arriving alongside the proposed "Regulation Crypto Assets" offering regime under Chairman Atkins' Project Crypto.
Custody is where self-sovereignty and regulation actually collide. This rule, not the offering regime, is the one that determines whether a DAO treasury can be held by its own multisig or must sit at a qualified custodian.
