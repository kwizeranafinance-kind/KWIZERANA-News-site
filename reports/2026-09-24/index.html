Matters

That means longs are closing, not new shorts opening. The ETF streak is intact, now five sessions and +$2.65B, but it is shrinking by roughly half each day. That is what a floor looks like while it is being tested, not while it is holding.

SIGNAL 03

Cosmos Hub halts for about 25 hours and moves $2.2M of ATOM away from the attacker behind a governance takeover of Neutron

Moved
An attacker used an “AI Agent Takeover” governance proposal to take control of Neutron on Sep 22 and drain Astroport and Drop contracts, a loss put at about $9.5M by Protos ($9.3M in CryptoSlate's earlier count). Cosmos Hub validators then halted their own chain for about 25 hours and, in the first block after restart, moved 1,227,121 ATOM (about $2.2M) out of an attacker-linked address (CryptoSlate Sep 24, corroborated by Protos Sep 23). A THORChain refund of 168,990 ATOM missed the sweep and was sold for about $266.8K USDC. Protos reports the governance votes cost the attacker about $20,199 to buy.
Matters
A parent chain halted and moved funds to reverse an attack on a different chain. That is a clawback precedent every Cosmos-ecosystem user now has to price in, both as protection and as a sign that validators can seize balances when they choose to. It is also the second governance-capture drain in about a month, after Term Finance on Aug 23. Both came through voting power, not a code bug. For any DAO whose token is thinly traded, “audited” no longer answers the security question: the attack price is the cost of a quorum.
Next
Neutron contributors plan a restart binary that restores the hijacked contracts, tightens governance and moves attacker-held assets to a validator multisig, and they aimed to restart within about 24 hours of Sep 23 (single-source: a former contributor cited by CryptoSlate). Falsifiable: if Neutron is not producing blocks by Sep 27, or the post-mortem does not state how Astroport and Drop users are made whole, the recovery was only partial. Because 168,990 ATOM have already been sold, full restitution needs a treasury backstop that nobody has named yet.
