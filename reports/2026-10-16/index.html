Pirate Chain Exploit Mints 39.8M ARRR, Rollback Plan Dropped
◆ Single-source The Crypto Times (Crypto Briefing and AMBCrypto relay the same project statements) · Oct 16, 2026 · source
About 39.8M unauthorized ARRR (roughly 20% of supply) were minted via a bypassed issuance check in the Ironwood turnstile accounting layer that went live Oct 3; the team dropped a rollback to Oct 14 20:00 UTC, notaries paused and the coins moved into the Sapling shielded pool. ✓ Verified Ledger company blog (primary) and The Crypto Times (Oct 13 and Oct 16 coverage) · Oct 15, 2026 · source
