Ledger is investigating possible wallet tampering after an investigator traced more than $86M of reported thefts, and has told buyers from the last 90 days not to set up their devices

79thVault Hack: $12.5M Drained From 79AU Pool on BNB Chain
◆ Single-source The Crypto Times (CertiK, Defimon, GoPlus and PeckShield attributions inside) · Oct 8, 2026 · source
About $12.5M (16,249 BNB) left the 79AU/USDT pool on Oct 7 via roughly 95 swaps after a single OPERATOR_ROLE wallet moved 2.01M 79AU tokens out and sold them for USDT. Why it matters: The role sat on one address with no multisig or timelock and the contract is unverified on BscScan, so this is a privileged-key or insider failure rather than a code bug.
