"Cold storage" is only as good as its entropy — and drain totals compound for days after disclosure. Verify seed provenance, not just device brand, on every KWIZERANA self-custody setup.
Adform ad-network JavaScript compromised Aug 1 — rewrites wallet addresses on legitimate sites
