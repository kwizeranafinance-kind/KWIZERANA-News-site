SIGNAL 01

Bitget's phased reopening meets a splitting laundering market: NEAR Intents and Chainflip turn the hackers away, THORChain does not

Moved
Bitget began phased withdrawals on Sep 28 (BTC first; ETH Sep 29, USDT Sep 30, other assets and P2P Oct 2) after a $387.5M hack (revised up from $351.6M), backed by a ~$464M protection fund; BGB fell about 5% to $1.95. Matters

Next
Bitget's own schedule is the test: ETH resumption today (Sep 29), USDT Sep 30 and the remaining assets plus P2P on Oct 2. A further upward loss revision or a paused phase falsifies the 'contained' reading; the Mandiant/SlowMist incident report Bitget promised for this week (about Oct 3-4) is the next hard data point.
