Privacy tech is only as strong as its least-private hop. For KWIZERANA's privacy pillar: assume the bridge, not the circuit, is where the audit budget goes.
AI is collapsing the window between vulnerability creation, discovery and exploitation
PYMNTS · Aug 2026 · pymnts.com
The argument is not that AI broke cryptography — it is that automated discovery has compressed the time from bug introduction to weaponised exploit, pushing institutions from periodic audits toward continuous testing.
A once-a-year audit is now a stale artefact. Any protocol treating an audit PDF as a security posture is measuring the wrong thing.
"The most terrifying bug of 2026 wasn't a hack — it was a broken assumption"
Medium · Jun–Aug 2026 · medium.com
A widely-circulated analysis arguing that 2026's worst losses came from unexamined assumptions in otherwise-correct code rather than from novel attack techniques.
Coldcard proves the thesis exactly: correct code, wrong assumption about entropy sources. Threat-model the assumptions, not just the implementation.

Key Trends

  • The self-custody supply chain is the new attack surface. Coldcard firmware, Trezor's shipping vendor, a broker breach — three separate failures in a fortnight, none of them requiring a single line of exploited smart-contract code.
  • Entropy failures are unpatchable and retroactive. A software bug gets fixed; a bad seed is permanently bad. This category of defect will drive a market for verifiable, auditable key generation.
  • ZK privacy holds; the edges leak. Aztec's circuits survived. The bridge did not. Every privacy design in 2026 is failing at the point where it touches a transparent chain.

Ones to Watch

  • Product-liability precedent from the Coldcard claims. If a court finds a hardware vendor liable for a cryptographic defect, the entire self-custody industry gets an insurance and disclosure regime overnight.
  • Continuous-testing tooling as a funded category. If AI really has compressed the exploit window, the audit-firm model breaks and monitoring-as-a-service replaces it. Watch for the first serious raise in this lane.
