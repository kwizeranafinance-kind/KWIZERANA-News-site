SIGNAL 03

Bittensor’s Finney chain moved to runtime 490 on Nov 10, fixing root-dividend settlement and adding Gamma, a way for subnets to pay each other out of emissions

Moved
Finney’s spec_version went from 488 to 490 at block 9,478,217 (20:25:48 UTC Nov 10), carrying the release-489 code (the 489 proposal was never signed). PR 3319 replaces the all-subnet price-stability rule for root dividends with a 0.1% basket-NAV impact limit plus 2% incoming-value and mint-ratio limits; PR 3320 lets subnet owners route up to 10% of emission per target into Gamma credits on other subnets. SN58 Attune is the first subnet to switch Gamma on. TAO is $219.18 (−0.36%, CoinGecko 13:15Z).
Matters
Emissions so far could only be split between owners, validators and miners; Gamma lets a subnet spend part of them to buy another subnet’s service, which creates an on-chain revenue line for service subnets that does not depend on the TAO price. The dividend fix unblocks root stakers whose payouts were deferred because a single small holding sat outside its price band. Checked on-chain and in the release notes by our researcher; no independent outlet coverage; whether deferred dividends have actually settled was not checked, and no Gamma emission allocation has been observed yet.
Next
By Nov 18: whether any subnet besides SN58 opts in and the first Gamma credits are minted on-chain (none observed at 13:20Z Nov 11); none would mean the feature has no demand yet. Today ~18:30–19:30Z: SN70 Fugal’s v2 config activates at block 9,484,920; Nov 16: SN89 InfiniteQuant’s 3% drawdown rule takes effect.
