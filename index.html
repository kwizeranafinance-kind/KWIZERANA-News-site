Zakura proposes per-swap viewing keys to shield ZEC swap history on NEAR Intents
◆ Single-source CCN · Oct 20, 2026 · source
The Oct 18 proposal gives each ZEC swap its own incoming viewing key, so one exposed refund address would not reveal a wallet’s full history; NEAR reported over $1.5B cumulative ZEC volume through Intents as of July. Why it matters: If adopted, it fixes a metadata leak at the cross-chain swap layer without a network upgrade, which is where much ZEC privacy loss to analytics firms occurs. Proposal only, not deployed; developer-authored; the quantum-risk framing is theoretical per the article.
Cysic bridge exploit mints 10M unbacked CYS on BNB Chain
✓ Verified CoinCentral, CryptoTicker · Oct 18, 2026 · source

Qubic halts chain after weak wallet seeds let attacker drain 1,404 wallets
✓ Verified crypto.news, CryptoTicker, Coingape · Oct 18, 2026 · source
Low-entropy recovery seeds from browser and iOS wallet software let an attacker drain about 2.94T QU (about $1.4M) from 1,404 wallets between Oct 17 and Oct 18. Why it matters: A client-side randomness bug means seeds already generated stay exposed after the fix, so the recovery vote, not the patch, determines final losses. Counts come from Qubic’s own statement; SlowMist lists the loss as $1.3M and outlets range $1.3M–$1.4M.

Pirate Chain exploit mints about 39.8M ARRR, raising supply about 20%
◆ Single-source Analytics Insight, CoinGabbar · Oct 16, 2026 · source
Two validation failures after the Ironwood upgrade allowed about 39.8M ARRR to be created into the shielded pool on Oct 14; rollback was dropped because the network is decentralised. Why it matters: Shielded-pool inflation cannot be audited from outside the pool, which is the specific supply-verification weakness privacy chains must solve with turnstile and supply-tracking checks. The 39.8M and 20% figures are project-reported, so capped at single; the project’s X post was not opened; SlowMist lists a $10.7M loss on an unknown basis; no post-mortem yet.
Ledger confirms hidden LTE implants in CryptoBilis-sold wallets leaked recovery phrases
✓ Verified The Record, BleepingComputer · Oct 13, 2026 · source
Implants captured 24-word phrases during setup and sent them over an eSIM LTE link; thefts began Oct 9, with third-party estimates of $86M–$93M. Why it matters: The attack bypasses the Secure Element and passes authenticity checks, so reseller-channel trust is now a custody risk; Trezor is cutting resellers (partnerships end Jan 1, 2027). Ledger has not confirmed a loss total: $86M is analyst Specter, $93M is a Help Net Security headline with no shown attribution. Bloomberg’s Oct 18 piece could not be fetched.
SlugFest flaw in npm Trusted Publishing exposed 1,500+ packages to takeover
✓ Verified The Hacker News, CSO Online · Oct 13, 2026 · source
Trusted Publishing bound packages to repository names instead of numeric IDs, so an attacker could claim an abandoned name; over 1,500 packages with over 10M combined downloads were exposed. Why it matters: Wallet SDKs and crypto libraries sit in this dependency graph, and the July Injective SDK backdoor shows how a single publish reaches key-handling code. Bloom Security saw no exploitation in the wild; reported to GitHub Jun 6 and fixed by npm Oct 2. Second source: csoonline.com/article/4234229.
