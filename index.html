XRP Ledger patched decade-old bug that could create billions of dollars in XRP from nothing
✓ Verified CoinDesk; Gizmodo (‘XRP Bug Could Have Let Hackers Print Billions in Crypto Out of Thin Air’) · Oct 10–11, 2026 · source
An integer overflow in the XRPL’s built-in exchange, likely dating to 2015, could have let a multi-offer trade mint XRP beyond the supply cap; researcher Cayden Liao and Veria AI reported it Sept 22 and the fix (xrpld 3.4.1) shipped Sept 25. Why it matters: The fix bypassed the usual validator vote, which Gizmodo says is a first, and it shows AI-assisted bug hunting reaching a ten-year-old codebase. RippleX’s ‘no evidence of exploitation’ is its own statement and is not independently verified.
France Targets Unreported Self-Custody Crypto Holdings Over €100K With Proposed €10K Fines
✓ Verified CoinGape; Crypto Briefing (‘French lawmakers advance proposal for €100K crypto wallet disclosure’) · Oct 12, 2026 · source
The Finance Committee advanced amendment I-CF821 on Oct 9, requiring annual declaration of self-custody wallets of €100K or more, while the related budget revenue section was rejected 31–3 and full-Assembly debate resumes Oct 13. Why it matters: It would be a direct reporting duty on self-hosted wallets in a major EU state, the opposite direction from FinCEN’s Oct 5 withdrawal. Not law; a €5K version was rejected in April 2026, and CoinGape’s page says ‘in 2027’, which conflicts with its date.
