OKX extended its March strategic round (led by ICE with about $200M) at an unchanged $25B pre-money valuation, adding Circle, Ripple, Qube Research & Technologies and SC Ventures, with the new amount undisclosed. Why it matters: Two rival stablecoin issuers taking equity in one exchange suggests distribution and USDC/RLUSD shelf space is being bought with equity. The new-round size is undisclosed.

Key Trends

  • Concentration up the stack — Series C in Q3 exceeded all of H1 and the top-five lead investors slowed to 2.0 deals a month from 3.7 (Tiger).
  • Stablecoin, RWA and payments are where the money is — Infrastructure’s share of disclosed investment rose from 8.1% to 18.2% while DeFi fell 71% to 3%; Spiko, Tazapay, BVNK and OKX fit the pattern.
  • Strategic and issuer capital replaces pure VC — Circle, Ripple, Tether, ICE and SC Ventures are writing the cheques; public exits are mostly M&A as IPOs pause.

Ones to Watch

  • Blockchain.com IPO (Rumor / Watch) — A confidential S-1 was reported in May (BeInCrypto); no public filing was seen this week, so timing is unconfirmed.
  • Circle/Tazapay closing — Expected in 2027, pending the Monetary Authority of Singapore; the $400M price is from the filing.
