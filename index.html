Matters
With about 31 open slots, a new subnet no longer has to push out an incumbent, so the deregistration threat that has disciplined weak subnets is suspended until the slots fill; cheaper locks speed that filling. Widening the emission gate from 32 to 48 moves issuance from the largest subnets to mid-ranked ones (one analyst’s model, Bittensor Intern via The TAO Daily: top-32 share about −6.2%, ranks 33–48 about +107%), which shrinks the emission base that funds the biggest subnets’ buybacks. Runtime 494 also moved to transaction version 2, so any wallet, indexer or signer that did not regenerate metadata has been broken since Monday afternoon.

South Korea draft bill backs bank-led stablecoins, 20% cap
✓ Verified GNcrypto (+ crypto.news Nov 23 preview; Bloomingbit citing Seoul Shinmun; CoinPaprika) · Nov 24, 2026 · source
The FSC's draft Digital Asset Basic Act limits won-stablecoin issuance at first to groups in which banks jointly own more than 50%, though a fintech may be the largest single holder. Issuers would need ₩5B (about $3.7M) in equity and 100% reserves redeemable at par. The draft caps an individual's stake in an exchange at 20% (the FSC could approve up to 34%) and would reopen ICOs, banned since 2017. Why it matters: Under a bank-majority structure, a won stablecoin would come from bank consortia rather than Kakao or Naver-type fintechs, and the Democratic Party wants the law passed before the US GENIUS Act takes effect on Jan 18, 2027. This is a draft that the FSC's Virtual Asset Committee discussed on Nov 24 at 3pm KST; it is not law and still faces National Assembly negotiation Reported exchange caps differ: crypto.news and CoinPaprika say 15–20%, GNcrypto says 20% with FSC approval up to 34% No FSC primary document opened

ECB expands acceptance of DLT-based assets as eligible Eurosystem collateral
✓ Verified ECB press release (primary) (+ crypto.news) · Nov 23, 2026 · source
From June 2027 the Governing Council will accept marketable assets issued on DLT Pilot Regime-licensed platforms as Eurosystem collateral. The assets need an eligible link to a CSD that settles on T2S, and detailed requirements are due in spring 2027. Why it matters: Once tokenized bonds can be pledged at the central bank, they can be funded at repo-like haircuts, which removes a liquidity discount that has held back EU on-chain issuance. Assets that are native to DLT with no CSD link remain excluded. Covers regulated securities only, not crypto-assets or stablecoins Settlement-finality and outsourcing requirements are still to be written
