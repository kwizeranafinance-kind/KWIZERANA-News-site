SIGNAL 03

The OCC finalized its first GENIUS Act rule on Oct 21 (effective Jan 18, 2027), dropping the presumption that affiliate rewards break the yield ban, a day before SoFi launched a 4.4% FDIC-covered tokenized deposit

Moved
OCC Bulletin 2026-50 adopts new 12 CFR Part 15 for stablecoin issuers and custody, effective Jan 18, 2027, replacing the proposal’s presumption against issuer-affiliate and third-party rewards with case-by-case review and moving reserves to a principles-based approach (The Block, Cointelegraph, Crypto Briefing). On Oct 22 SoFi launched SoFi Earn (SOFIE), a variable 4.4% APY tokenized deposit convertible 1:1 into its SoFiUSD stablecoin (Cointelegraph, Ledger Insights). Stablecoin supply is $304.93B on our DefiLlama sum.
Matters
Platform rewards such as PayPal’s 4% on PYUSD, Coinbase One’s 3.75% on USDC and Kraken+ up to 4.5% on USDG are legally unsettled until a court or later rule decides them, while banks are competing for idle balances with tokenized deposits that sit outside the payment-stablecoin definition. Treasury’s AML rules and the Fed, FDIC and Treasury GENIUS rules are not final; SoFiUSD itself carries no FDIC insurance and 4.4% is a variable, issuer-supplied rate.
Next
Whether the OCC announces an issuer application or waiver intake date (Bulletins 2026-51 and 2026-52 cover forms), and whether the Fed, FDIC or Treasury finalize their rules before the Jan 18, 2027 effective date. SoFi’s SOFIE rollout to all SoFi Crypto members is scheduled through November.

Coinbase Ireland fined $3.6 million over customer fund segregation, oversight failures
◆ Single-source The Block · Oct 22, 2026 · source

OCC finalizes GENIUS Act rules for stablecoin issuers and custody
✓ Verified OCC Bulletin 2026-50 (primary); The Block; Cointelegraph; Crypto Briefing · Oct 21, 2026 · source
The OCC issued the first final GENIUS Act rule (new 12 CFR Part 15), effective Jan 18, 2027, dropping the proposal’s presumption that issuer-affiliate or third-party rewards violate the yield ban and moving reserves to a principles-based approach. Why it matters: Case-by-case review of platform rewards (PayPal 4% on PYUSD, Coinbase One 3.75% on USDC, Kraken+ up to 4.5% on USDG) leaves yield legally unsettled until courts or a later rule, which analysts such as TD Cowen call a negative for banks.
